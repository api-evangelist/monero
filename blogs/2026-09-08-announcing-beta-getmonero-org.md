Work on the redesign began with a concept by rehrar (Diego Salazar), shared in Figma in 2024 and refined through community feedback. Building it out was funded through the CCS , and the result is now up for testing: beta.getmonero.org The beta front page. The rules the beta is built against: No JavaScript required No landing-page gimmicks No dark-only theme Designed for mobile from the start No increase in page weight What is new in the beta Taking those rules in order.
